ShopEase Payment & Billing Policy

1. Supported Payment Modes:
Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), Debit Cards, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

2. Double Debits & Failed Transactions:
In the event that an amount is debited from the customer's account or card but the order status displays 'Payment Failed' or 'Order Pending', payment gateways typically auto-reconcile within 24 to 48 hours. If funds are not auto-reversed within 3 business days, provide the transaction reference ID (UTR or Gateway Ref ID) for manual finance reconciliation.

3. Payment Gateway Timeouts:
Payment timeouts during peak sale windows are investigated by the Finance operations team. Once verified, either order fulfillment is initiated or a prompt refund is issued.

4. Fraud Protection & Security:
All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption and mandatory multi-factor authentication (OTP/3DSecure).
