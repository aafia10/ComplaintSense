ShopEase Damaged or Defective Product Policy

1. Reporting Timeframe:
Any item received in a damaged, broken, defective, or tampered condition must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.

2. Evidence Required:
Customers are requested to provide clear photographs or video evidence showing the outer packaging damage, shipping label with Order ID (#ORDxxxxx), and the defect on the physical item.

3. Resolution Options:
- Immediate Zero-Cost Replacement: Dispatched within 24 hours of report validation.
- Full Refund: Processed immediately if replacement inventory is out of stock.
- Technical Inspection: For high-value electronics (smartphones, laptops), a certified technician may conduct a remote or on-site diagnosis.

4. Logistics Liability:
If transit damage occurred via our delivery partners (such as SpeedFast Logistics), ShopEase files a transit damage claim and shields the customer from any liability.
