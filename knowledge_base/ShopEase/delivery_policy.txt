ShopEase Delivery & Shipping Policy

1. Delivery Timelines:
- Standard Delivery: 3 to 5 business days across metro cities and 5 to 7 days for tier-2/3 regions.
- Express Delivery: 24 to 48 hours for eligible pin codes in metropolitan hubs such as Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru.

2. Logistics Partners:
Shipments are fulfilled through accredited courier services including SpeedFast Logistics, BlueLine Air, and ShopEase Direct Express. When shipments enter regional sorting hubs (e.g. Mumbai Hub), tracking updates reflect live status.

3. Delays & Escalations:
If a package is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date by more than 48 hours, an automated investigation ticket is raised with the logistics operations team. In cases of chronic delays exceeding 5 days, customers are offered a full refund or immediate priority dispatch of a replacement order.

4. Delivery Address Modifications:
Address changes can only be accommodated within 2 hours of order placement before shipment handoff to the courier hub.
