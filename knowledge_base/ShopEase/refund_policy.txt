ShopEase Refund Policy

1. Eligibility for Refunds:
Customers are entitled to a full refund if an order is cancelled prior to shipment, if a returned item is received in its original condition within 14 days of delivery, or if a purchased product is verified as damaged or defective upon arrival.

2. Refund Processing Timeline:
Once approved, refunds are credited back to the original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, or Net Banking) within 5 to 7 business days. For payments made via Cash on Delivery (COD), customers must provide their bank account details for a direct NEFT transfer.

3. Partial Refunds:
Partial refunds may be granted for items returned with missing accessories, packaging, or tags, or if delivery delays exceeded guaranteed express delivery windows by more than 72 hours.

4. Non-Refundable Items:
Intimate apparel, perishable items, digital vouchers, and clearance sale items marked as 'Final Sale' are not eligible for monetary refunds unless defective upon receipt.

5. How to Request:
Submit a ticket under Billing & Refund with your Order ID (#ORDxxxxx) and bank transaction reference.
