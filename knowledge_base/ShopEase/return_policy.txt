ShopEase Return Policy

1. Return Window:
Customers may initiate a return request within 14 calendar days from the date of product delivery.

2. Condition of Returned Items:
Items must be unused, unwashed, in the original condition received, with all manufacturer tags intact and in the original packaging. Electronic products must include all original cords, manuals, and accessories.

3. Return Pickup Process:
Upon initiating a return, ShopEase logistics will schedule a free doorstep pickup within 48 to 72 hours. An inspection is performed at the doorstep before acceptance.

4. Replacement vs Refund:
Customers may select either an exact item replacement (subject to inventory availability) or a store credit/original payment refund.

5. Exceptions:
Custom-built items, software licenses, and personal hygiene goods are exempt from standard returns unless verified defective by technical support.
