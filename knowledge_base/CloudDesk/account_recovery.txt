CloudDesk Account Recovery & Access Security Policy

1. Forgotten Passwords & MFA Reset:
Users can initiate self-service password reset using registered primary corporate emails. If 2FA/MFA authenticator app access is lost, organizational workspace admins can issue a temporary recovery security token.

2. SSO / SAML Lockouts:
For enterprise SSO integrations (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace), SSO errors must be coordinated with your internal IT security administrator. CloudDesk support can temporarily enable break-glass password authentication upon verification of company domain ownership.

3. Locked Accounts:
Accounts undergo automatic lockout following 5 consecutive failed login attempts. Unlocking occurs automatically after 30 minutes, or instantly via the emailed security confirmation link.
