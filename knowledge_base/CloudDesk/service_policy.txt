CloudDesk Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Uptime Policy

1. Guaranteed Service Availability:
CloudDesk guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for all Enterprise tier production services, excluding scheduled maintenance windows notified at least 72 hours in advance.

2. SLA Service Credits:
In the event that monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, customers are eligible for service credits on their subsequent subscription invoice:
- 99.0% to 99.89%: 10% credit
- 95.0% to 98.99%: 25% credit
- Below 95.0%: 50% credit

3. Data Retention & Backups:
Continuous snapshots are retained for 30 days. Customers may export workspace data in JSON/CSV formats at any time.
