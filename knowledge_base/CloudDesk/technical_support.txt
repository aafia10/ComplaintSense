CloudDesk Technical Support & Incident Escalation Policy

1. Support Tiers:
- Standard Support (Starter & Pro): Web ticket and email support with responses within 8 business hours.
- Premium 24/7 Support (Enterprise): Dedicated account manager, private Slack/Teams channel, and guaranteed 1-hour critical response SLA.

2. Bug Reporting & Diagnostics:
When submitting technical issues regarding API timeouts, dashboard blank screens, or webhook drops, provide: browser version, developer console logs, error request IDs, and timestamp in UTC.

3. Engineering Escalation:
Critical production outages (Sev-1) are routed automatically to on-call infrastructure engineering. Regular software defect patches are released on bi-weekly deployment cycles.
