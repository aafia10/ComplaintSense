ConnectTel Prepaid Recharge & Payment Policy

1. Recharge Execution SLA:
Digital prepaid recharges completed via UPI, debit card, or net banking should reflect on the subscriber account within 15 minutes.

2. Failed Recharges with Bank Deduction:
If your bank account was debited but the prepaid pack or data allowance was not credited to your ConnectTel number, the banking network normally auto-reconciles within 24 hours. Provide your bank UTR number and mobile number if the recharge status remains unfulfilled.

3. Plan Expiry & Grace Periods:
Incoming calls remain active for 7 days post plan expiration. Unused data roll-over benefits apply only when renewing an active pack prior to the midnight expiry threshold.
