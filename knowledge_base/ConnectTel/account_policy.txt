ConnectTel Subscriber Account & SIM Management Policy

1. SIM Replacement & eSIM Migration:
Subscribers can request an instant eSIM profile transfer or replacement physical SIM card at any authorized ConnectTel store with government photo identification.

2. Mobile Number Portability (MNP):
To port an existing number to ConnectTel or another operator, send 'PORT <10-digit-mobile-number>' to 1900. Porting codes remain valid for 4 days.

3. Account Ownership Transfer:
Ownership transfer between corporate entities or family members requires biometric KYC re-verification and clearance of all outstanding postpaid balances.
