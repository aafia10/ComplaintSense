ConnectTel Postpaid Billing & Tariff Dispute Policy

1. Billing Cycle & Due Dates:
ConnectTel postpaid accounts operate on a monthly billing cycle closing on the 15th or 30th of each month, with payment due 15 days following invoice generation.

2. Disputed Charges & International Roaming:
If unauthorized data usage, roaming charges, or premium value-added service (VAS) subscriptions appear on your statement, submit a Billing & Refund complaint with your subscriber phone number (#TEL-XXXXX). Billed amounts under active dispute are quarantined from service suspension during investigation.

3. Reconciliation & Credit Adjustments:
Valid billing errors are credited directly to the subscriber's subsequent monthly invoice within 72 hours of verification.
