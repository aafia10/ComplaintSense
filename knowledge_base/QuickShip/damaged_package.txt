QuickShip Damaged Freight Policy

1. Reporting Damage:
Consignees must inspect outer parcels upon delivery. If physical damage, crush signs, or seal tampering is noticed, note 'Damaged on Arrival' on the digital POD (Proof of Delivery) scanner.

2. Claims Submission:
Claims for damaged cargo must be lodged within 72 hours of delivery accompanied by images of damaged outer cartons, internal bubble wrapping, and the affected merchandise.

3. Damage Inspection:
A QuickShip claims surveyor reviews photographic evidence within 24 hours to authorize commercial compensation.
